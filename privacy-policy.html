Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information Haider Commercial Traders (“we”, “us”) collects when you visit haider-traders.com or contact us, and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Details you give us when you call, message us on WhatsApp or email us, such as your name, phone number, village or location, the machine or part you want, and your crop and acreage.
  • Photos you send us, for example a photo of a spare part.
  • Basic technical data that your browser and our website host may record automatically, such as your IP address and the pages visited.

This website has no user accounts and does not take online payments, so we do not collect card or bank details through it.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and give you a price or quote.
  • To arrange a viewing, a sale, a harvesting job or delivery of parts.
  • To keep records of our sales and services.
  • To keep the website working and secure.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only when needed to complete your request (for example with a driver delivering a part), when the law requires it, or with service providers that run our website and messaging. WhatsApp and Google are separate services with their own privacy policies.

Cookies and analytics

We do not intentionally set tracking cookies. If we add analytics or advertising tools in future, we will update this page.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and sales records for as long as needed to serve you, keep accounts and meet legal duties, then delete them or make them anonymous.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no method of sending data over the internet is completely secure.

Your choices

You can ask us to tell you what information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. Contact us using the details below.

Children

Our website and services are meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
